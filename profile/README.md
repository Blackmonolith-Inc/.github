# Blackmonolith, Inc.

Blackmonolith builds consumer AI products that turn personal context into shareable, emotionally clear experiences.

Our current focus is **[Sonder](https://thesonder.ai/)**, a relationship intelligence product designed to make complex compatibility and relationship readings simple enough to share, compare, and discuss.

## What we are building

- AI-assisted relationship and compatibility experiences
- Mobile-first consumer product flows
- Backend systems for identity, content generation, and personalization
- Web surfaces for product discovery and sharing

## Repositories

Most active product repositories are private while the product is in development. Public repositories and release-ready packages will appear here when they are ready for external use.
