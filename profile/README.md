# Blackmonolith, Inc.

Blackmonolith builds AI-native products that turn complex workflows and personal context into clear, useful experiences.

## Products

### PolyDraft

**PolyDraft** is an [AI Website Builder](https://polydraft.ai/) that helps businesses create, publish, and continuously improve production websites for SEO, GEO, AI visibility, and localization.

### Sonder

**[Sonder](https://thesonder.ai/)** is a relationship intelligence product designed to make complex compatibility and relationship readings simple enough to share, compare, and discuss.

## What we are building

- AI-native products for consumers and businesses
- AI-assisted website creation, SEO, GEO, and localization
- Mobile-first consumer product experiences
- Backend systems for identity, content generation, and personalization
- Web surfaces for product discovery, publishing, and sharing
